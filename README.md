# public

## Taper Tracker

A free, private web app that estimates buprenorphine (Suboxone) receptor occupancy from your dose history and shows it live, to help you see your progress while tapering.

- **[⬇ Download Taper Tracker (ZIP file)](https://github.com/Biggetit/public/archive/refs/heads/claude/suboxone-occupancy-timer-sds4cl.zip)**
- **[⬇ Download just the app file (index.html)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Biggetit/public/claude/suboxone-occupancy-timer-sds4cl/suboxone-occupancy/index.html)**: right-click and choose **Save link as…**
- **[Start here: how to download, use and keep your data](suboxone-occupancy/README.md)**

No account, installs or coding needed. Your data stays in your own browser.
